01 — Shipping & Delivery

Shipping & Delivery

Nim's Atelier currently ships exclusively within India.

Delivery Information

  • India-wide delivery is available.
  • Flat delivery charge of ₹150 - FREE above ₹5000.
  • Tracking information will be provided where available.
  • Delivery time depends on the destination.
Please note: Nim's Atelier is not responsible for delays caused by courier services.
02 — Payment Options

Payment Options

All prices on Nim's Atelier are displayed in Indian Rupees (₹). We offer secure and easy payment methods for your convenience.

Available Payment Methods

  • UPI - Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM & all UPI apps
  • Net Banking - All major banks supported
  • Cards - Credit Card & Debit Card
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) - Pay when your order arrives

Payment Security

Your payment details are processed through secure payment gateways. Nim's Atelier does not store your card / UPI information.

Note: COD may not be available for customized and bridal orders. Additional COD charge of ₹200 may apply on some orders.
03 — Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and aim to handle customer information responsibly.

Information We May Collect

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Shipping address
  • Order information

How Information May Be Used

  • Processing and delivering orders.
  • Communicating about orders.
  • Providing customer support.

We do not intentionally sell customer personal information to third parties.

04 — Refund Policy

Refund Policy

Order Cancellation

Cancellation requests should be made as soon as possible after placing an order. Once an item has entered production, cancellation may no longer be possible.

Refunds

  • Approved refunds will be processed through the original payment method.
  • UPI / Card refunds: 5-7 working days
  • Net Banking refunds: 7-10 working days
  • COD orders refunded via bank transfer / store credit
Important: Customized and made-to-order products may not qualify for refund unless they arrive damaged.

Terms of Service

Pricing
